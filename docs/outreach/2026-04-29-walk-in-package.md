# Boston Drop-Off Package — Wed/Thu Walk-Ins

For the 5 in-person Boston firm visits (Wed 2026-05-06 + Thu 2026-05-07, 10am–2pm). **Reframe:** this is *not* a cold drop-in to meet the principal. This is delivering a printed package to the front desk for them to pass along.

## What to bring (printed in the morning before walking out)

For each of the 5 firms, prepare:

1. **Printed LOI** (1 page, print `0studio-design-partner-loi.pdf` directly — recipient can sign on paper at the desk and email a photo back, or sign in Preview if they prefer digital)
2. **1-page thesis printout** — print `0studio.xyz/thesis` to PDF then to paper, or a custom 1-page summary
3. **Hand-written note** on a small card (see template below)
4. **Business card** with `founders@0studio.xyz` + `0studio.xyz`

Stack as: card on top → note → thesis → LOI. Paper-clip together. Hand-deliver to reception in a plain manila envelope addressed `Attn: {Principal Name}, {Firm}`.

## Hand-written note template (~50 words, write fresh per firm — don't print)

> {Principal first name},
>
> Came by hoping to drop this off — I'm a Boston founder building 0studio,
> version control for Rhino + Revit files. Would mean a lot to have {Firm}
> as a design partner. The LOI is non-binding, takes 30 seconds.
>
> Email me anytime: founders@0studio.xyz · 0studio.xyz/thesis
>
> — Colin Kim (MIT B.Arch '{year})

> Tip: write the principal's name slightly larger at the top so reception flags it correctly. Keep your handwriting legible.

## Office-hours visit script (when reception asks who you are)

> "Hi — I'm Colin Kim, I have a small package I'd like to leave for {Principal Name}. Don't need to wait, just want to make sure they get it. Thanks."

If asked what it's about: "It's a quick design-partner introduction for a tool I'm building." Keep it short. Don't pitch reception — they're not the buyer.

Pick 5 from your BSA-sourced list within 1 mile of each other. Suggested clusters:

**Back Bay / South End cluster:** Höweler + Yoon (Boylston-area), NADAAA (Newbury-area), Utile, Studio Luz, Anmahian Winton (Cambridge — slight drive)

Walk one cluster Wednesday, the other Thursday. Park once, walk between.
