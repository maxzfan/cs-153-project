# Warm-Network LinkedIn DM Template

For the 5 warm-network architects (MIT B.Arch peers, GSD/GSAPP friends, prior-firm colleagues). Send Tuesday 2026-05-05 9am EST. **Ask for the LOI directly as a friend ask, not as a pitch.**

The right rhythm for a warm DM is shorter than the cold email. No subject line (LinkedIn DMs don't have one). Use first names you actually use.

---

## Template (~75 words)

```
Hey {first_name} —

Quick favor ask. I'm building 0studio (version control for Rhino + Revit
files, hopefully puts an end to "final_FINAL_v3.3dm" hell). Trying to
land 5 design-partner firms in Boston/NYC this week to anchor the launch
narrative.

Would you sign a 1-pager LOI as a design partner? Non-binding, takes 30
seconds — happy to email you the PDF, you sign in Preview and email
back. No money, no commitment — just expressing interest so I can say
"5 firms are in" when I'm pitching.

Thesis if useful: 0studio.xyz/thesis. Thanks either way.

— Colin
```

> When they say yes, reply with the LOI PDF attached + a one-liner: "Open in Preview, click Tools → Annotate → Signature, then send back. Takes 30 sec."

---

## Variants for context

**If they're at a firm where they're not the deciding partner:**
> "...the ask is: would you forward this to {Name} or whoever decides on tooling at {firm}? An LOI from {firm} would mean a lot."

**If they're solo or running a small studio:**
> "...you'd literally just sign as the firm — takes 30 seconds."

**If you haven't talked to them in 1+ year:**
> Add as opener: "It's been a minute — last time was {specific past context}. Hope {something specific to them: their child, their move, their firm}."

---

## Send rules

- One DM at a time, with intentional 5-min gaps (LinkedIn rate-limits cold patterns)
- Don't send all 5 in a 30-min block — spread across Tuesday morning
- If LinkedIn requires a connection request first, send the request with a 250-char note (use the message body shortened)
- Track in same spreadsheet, `source` column = `warm-DM`
