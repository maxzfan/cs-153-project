# 0studio Demo Video — Script & Framework

**Duration:** ~2 minutes
**Audience:** Potential customers (architects, designers at AEC firms) and investors
**Tone:** Confident, clean, fast-paced. Show, don't tell. Let the product breathe.

---

## Framework: The 5-Beat Structure

| Beat | Purpose | Duration | Principle |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| 1. The Problem | Emotional hook — the pain they already feel | 15s | Name the enemy |
| 2. The Product | First look — what is this? | 10s | One sentence, then show it |
| 3. The Core Loop | The thing you do every day in 0studio | 45s | Hands-on, real model, fast |
| 4. The Team Moment | Why this isn't just for solo work | 30s | Multiplayer changes the pitch |
| 5. The Close | What to do next | 15s | CTA, pricing anchor, confidence |

**Total: ~115 seconds** (leaves breathing room for transitions)

---

## Script

### Beat 1: The Problem (0:00–0:15)

**[SCREEN: Quick cuts of a messy Finder window — dozens of files named `Building_v3_final_FINAL2_john.3dm`. Then a Slack message: "which one is the latest?" Then a confused emoji reaction.]**

> **VO:** "Every architecture firm has a folder that looks like this. Dozens of Rhino files. No one knows which is latest. Someone overwrites someone else's work. Version control exists for code — but not for design. Until now."

---

### Beat 2: The Product (0:15–0:25)

**[SCREEN: 0studio opens. Clean dark UI. A real architectural model loads in the 3D viewport — orbiting slowly. The sidebar shows a commit tree with branches.]**

> **VO:** "This is 0studio — version control built for 3D design. Open your .3dm file, and every change is tracked, branched, and synced to your team."

---

### Beat 3: The Core Loop (0:25–1:10)

This is the meat. Move fast, show real actions. No filler.

**[SCREEN: User opens a Rhino file from the welcome panel. Model loads instantly in the viewer.]**

> **VO:** "Open any Rhino file."

**[SCREEN: User types a commit message — "Massing study v1" — and clicks Save Version. The commit appears in the sidebar tree.]**

> **VO:** "Save a version with one click. Every commit is a full snapshot you can always go back to."

**[SCREEN: User clicks "New Branch." Types "Option B — taller lobby." Makes a change in Rhino. Switches back to 0studio, commits again. The tree now shows two branches diverging.]**

> **VO:** "Branch to explore options without losing your main line. Each design direction lives in its own branch."

**[SCREEN: User clicks a starred commit from 3 days ago. The 3D model instantly swaps to that older version. Camera orbits to show the difference.]**

> **VO:** "Jump to any version instantly. Star the milestones that matter."

**[SCREEN: User enters Gallery mode. Selects 3 commits. Three viewports appear side by side showing different massing options.]**

> **VO:** "Compare up to four versions side by side in gallery mode. Perfect for design reviews."

**[SCREEN: User clicks the snapshot button (top-right overlay). Brief checkmark. Cuts to Slack — user pastes the screenshot directly into a channel.]**

> **VO:** "One click to copy a viewport screenshot to your clipboard. Paste it anywhere."

---

### Beat 4: The Team Moment (1:10–1:40)

**[SCREEN: User clicks "Push to Cloud." Progress indicator. Then the presence panel lights up — two teammates appear with colored avatars. One is viewing a different commit (shown by avatar on the tree).]**

> **VO:** "Push your history to the cloud. Your whole team sees the same project — who's online, what they're looking at, in real time."

**[SCREEN: Teammate's avatar moves to a different commit. A cloud icon appears on new commits.]**

> **VO:** "When a teammate pushes new versions, pull them down with one click. No more 'which file is latest' on Slack."

**[SCREEN: Settings page — add team member by email. Role dropdown shows Owner / Editor / Viewer.]**

> **VO:** "Invite your team by email. Control who can edit and who can view."

---

### Beat 5: The Close (1:40–1:55)

**[SCREEN: Quick montage — dark theme, light theme, graph view, list view, a beautiful architectural model rotating in the viewer. End on the 0studio logo centered on screen.]**

> **VO:** "0studio. Version control for architects. Free for solo use. Pro plans start at $10 a month."

**[SCREEN: Logo + URL + "Try it free" CTA]**

> **VO:** "Try it free at 0studio.app."

## Production Notes

### Visuals
- **Use a real architectural model** — something recognizable (massing study, facade, residential). Avoid toy geometry.
- **Dark theme** for the main demo — it reads better on video and looks more professional.
- **Screen recording at 2x resolution** (Retina) — export at 1080p or 4K.
- **Subtle zoom/pan** on screen recordings to direct attention — don't just show a static screen.
- **No cursor highlighting plugins** — they look cheap. A natural cursor is fine.

### Audio
- **VO should be warm but brisk** — not salesy, not monotone. Think "smart friend showing you something cool."
- **Background music:** Low, ambient, architectural. Something from Epidemic Sound or Artlist in the "minimal / electronic" category. Volume at ~15% under VO.
- **No sound effects** on clicks — they feel gimmicky in B2B.

### Pacing
- Never linger on one screen more than 5 seconds
- Every sentence should accompany a visible action
- Cut on the action, not after it — the viewer's brain fills in the rest
- The gallery mode comparison is the visual climax — give it an extra beat

### What NOT to show
- Billing/checkout UI (boring, erodes the magic)
- Error states or loading spinners
- Settings page details (except team invite — that's a feature)
- Terminal, code, or developer tooling
- Auth/signup flow (just start logged in)

### Key moments to nail
1. **The branching tree** — this is the hero visual. Make sure it has 5-8 commits across 2-3 branches so it looks rich but not cluttered.
2. **The gallery comparison** — use visually distinct design options (different massing, different heights) so the difference is obvious at a glance.
3. **The presence panel** — needs to feel alive. Two teammates with status messages like "Reviewing facade options."
4. **The snapshot paste** — the clipboard-to-Slack moment is the "wow, that's useful" beat. Don't rush it.
